Parking

Parking is a big issue in Jim Thorpe

The town is so narrow between the mountains that the Works Progress Administration under FDR built a tunnel for the creek, as there wasn't even room for a creek. Therefore parking is limited. We do have parking planned for the weekend for all of our guests, but we will need to know the arrival date. More info will follow.
