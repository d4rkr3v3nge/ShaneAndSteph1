Things To Do

Jim Thorpe is one of the most charming towns in Pennsylvania — historic streets, scenic overlooks, cozy shops, great food, and beautiful places to stay. Here are our favorite recommendations for your visit.

Activities

1. Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway

Vintage train rides through the mountains — perfect for sightseeing and photos.

Visit Website

2. Explore Historic Downtown

Walk Broadway, grab coffee, browse boutiques, and enjoy Victorian architecture.

3. Asa Packer Mansion

A beautifully preserved 1800s mansion with guided tours — a Jim Thorpe must‑see.

Learn More

4. Glen Onoko Falls Trail

One of the most scenic hikes in the region. Wear good shoes — the views are worth it.

5. Mauch Chunk Opera House

Historic theater with concerts and shows. Check the schedule — you might catch something fun.

See Events

Restaurants

1. Marion Hose Co.

Historic firehouse turned restaurant — great cocktails, great food, and a unique atmosphere.

Visit Website

2. Stone Row Pub & Eatery

Farm‑to‑table dishes, cocktails, and a cozy atmosphere. Great for dinner.

3. Molly Maguires Pub

Classic American comfort food and drinks right in the center of town.

4. Broadway Grille

Popular spot with a large menu, great drinks, and a lively vibe.

5. Café Arielle at the Stabin Museum

Unique artistic setting with great food — a hidden gem.

6. Donerds Donuts

Fun gourmet donuts and coffee — perfect for breakfast or a quick treat.
