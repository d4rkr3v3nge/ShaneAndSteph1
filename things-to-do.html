Things To Do in Jim Thorpe

  • Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway – Beautiful train rides through the gorge.

  • Hiking & Biking – Trails along the Lehigh Gorge and surrounding mountains.
  • Mansions & Museums – Harry Packer Mansion, Asa Packer Mansion, and more.
